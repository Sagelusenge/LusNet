## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, next-generation telecom interface engineered for high-bandwidth connectivity and mission-critical network reliability across Goma and the surrounding Great Lakes region. The aesthetic bridges physical telecom infrastructure—microwave towers, fiber conduits, and point-to-point wireless arrays—with the ethereal speed of photonics and digital signals.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Technical Glassmorphism & High-Precision Infrastructure**. It pairs deep midnight bedrock canvases with electric photonic blue highlights, ultra-fine technical borders, and frosted structural panels. The user experience evokes absolute stability, high throughput, and institutional trust, steering away from generic consumer ISP tropes toward enterprise-grade clarity and regional leadership.

## Colors

The color system operates on high-contrast kinetic balance. Deep dark surfaces anchor core technical hero blocks and premium product tiers, while crisp, sterile slate-white planes ensure effortless readability for customer portal workflows and billing breakdowns.

- **Primary (`#0A58CA`)**: The foundational carrier blue. Communicates stability, institutional strength, and core backbone throughput. Used for primary interactive actions and authoritative headers.
- **Secondary (`#00D2FF`)**: Electric photonic cyan. Represents active radio links, beam paths, ping animations, and key metrics. Applied as accents, interactive focus rings, and glowing edge-highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep atmospheric slate. Used as the dominant surface color for immersive network sections, dense tabular overlays, terminal headers, and enterprise tiers.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Cool structural gray. Provides balanced visual hierarchy for secondary copy, technical specs, network latency captions, and subtle separators.
- **Named Colors**:
  - `network-active` (`#10B981`): Real-time online status, healthy packet delivery, and uptime confirmations.
  - `network-idle` (`#F59E0B`): Congestion warnings, maintenance windows, and pending provisioning.
  - `network-down` (`#EF4444`): Outages, packet loss warnings, and disconnected CPE equipment.
  - `surface-dark` (`#0B132B`): Bedrock canvas for night-mode contexts and the telecom infrastructure hero.
  - `surface-light` (`#F8FAFC`): Base canvas for consumer onboarding and account self-care.

## Typography

The type stack combines the geometric dynamism and modern curves of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the utilitarian precision of **Inter**. 

- **Display & Headings**: Rendered in Plus Jakarta Sans with tightly kerned tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`). This conveys mechanical authority and high technological refinement.
- **Body Text**: Kept in Plus Jakarta Sans for welcoming, fatigue-free reading across billing portals, setup documentation, and service catalogs.
- **Labels & Network Telemetry**: Utilizes Inter with elevated letter-spacing. All latency markers, uplink/downlink rates (e.g., `Mbps`, `Gbps`, `GHz`), IP configurations, and hardware indicators are set in Inter to guarantee absolute legibility under critical observation.

## Layout & Spacing

The interface utilizes a precision 12-column fluid grid system pinned to an 8pt architectural cadence. Sections transition seamlessly between condensed technical instrument panels and expansive marketing layouts.

- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column structure with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Cards snap to 100% horizontal viewport minus boundary margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Pricing plans and telemetry indicators stack in 2-column configurations.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout capped at an inner maximum container width of `1280px`. Margins expand to `4rem`, with gutters at `2rem` to allow high visual separation between simultaneous metric streams.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages high-tech structural layering combining frosted backplates with ambient, electric-tinted photonics:

- **Level 0 (Floor)**: `#F8FAFC` on light customer canvases; `#0B132B` on infrastructure and monitoring dashboards.
- **Level 1 (Substrate Cards)**: Surface opacity at `85%` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Bounded by hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)` on light mode, `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)` on dark mode).
- **Level 2 (Active Hubs & Featured Tiers)**: Card surfaces elevate using ambient cyan luminescence: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(10, 88, 202, 0.12), 0 0 0 1px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.3)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Command Overlays)**: Heavy frosted dark glass (`rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.85)` with `24px` backdrop blur) coupled with a diffused edge glow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(2, 6, 23, 0.5), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced structural geometry (`roundedness: 2` / base 8px). This radius maintains architectural integrity suitable for industrial telecommunications while softening touch targets for standard users.

- **Core Buttons & Inputs**: `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius.
- **Service & Hardware Cards**: `1rem` (16px) corner radius with unified 1px border frames.
- **Badges, Metric Chips & Network Pills**: Fully pill-rounded (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish real-time dynamic states from static layout blocks.

## Components

### Action Buttons
- **Primary Telecom Action**: Vibrant blue fill (`#0A58CA`) with white type, 8px radius, and an active cyan hover glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.45)`). Transition duration set strictly to `150ms ease-out`.
- **Secondary / Fiber Diagnostics**: Semi-transparent surface (`rgba(10, 88, 202, 0.08)`) with a crisp 1px border (`#0284C7`), shifting to a solid border with text color brightening on focus.

### Status Indicators & Badges
- **Operational Badge ("Réseau opérationnel")**: Pill shape with low-opacity emerald background (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`), text in `#10B981`, accompanied by a pulsing hardware dot indicator featuring a radial glow keyframe animation.
- **Bandwidth Chips**: High-contrast, micro-padded pill chips (`Inter`, label-technical) denoting connection metrics (e.g., `5 Mbps`, `Point-to-Point`, `Fiber`).

### Service Pricing Matrix
- Three-tier card structure:
  - **Basic Home (5 Mbps / $15)**: Clean `#FFFFFF` base, subtle `#E2E8F0` border, muted metrics.
  - **Stream Plus (10 Mbps / $20)**: Highlit with a `2px` top border in `#00D2FF` and subtle internal blue gradient sheen.
  - **Pro Ultra (30 Mbps / $50)**: Enterprise-tier inverted dark card (`#0F172A`), electric blue typography accents, glowing primary CTA, and dedicated SLA badge.

### 4-Stage Deployment Timeline
- Horizontal tracking line on desktop, converting to vertical rail on mobile.
- Steps: *1. Éligibilité & Cartographie*, *2. Installation Antenne/Fibre*, *3. Configuration Équipement*, *4. Activation Haute Vitesse*.
- Completed and active steps utilize illuminated `#00D2FF` node connectors with subtle signal ping halos; pending steps remain in neutral `#64748B` outlines.

### Inputs & Portal Controls
- Form inputs feature cool slate backgrounds (`#F8FAFC`), structural borders (`#CBD5E1`), and shift to a dual-ring highlight (`#0A58CA` with an outer `#00D2FF` 3px blur) when activated.
